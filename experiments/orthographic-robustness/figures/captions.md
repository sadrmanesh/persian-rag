# Figure captions

All figures are provided as 300-dpi PNG and vector PDF (fonts embedded). Palette: Okabe–Ito (colour-blind safe); heatmaps use the perceptually uniform, colour-blind-safe *cividis* map. Author metadata: Mohammad Naser Sadrmanesh.

**Figure 1 — `fig1_architecture.(png|pdf)`.** Retrieval pipeline of persian-rag as evaluated in this study. A (possibly noisy) query is normalised with N_PR (NFKC, Arabic→Persian letter folding, digit and punctuation normalisation, ZWNJ/half-space correction; the same normaliser is applied to passages at indexing time) and sent to BM25 (k1 = 1.5, b = 0.75), a dense encoder (mE5-small/-base, BGE-M3 dense, or MiniLM-L12) and/or the character 2–4-gram hashing embedder. Ranked lists are combined with reciprocal rank fusion (k = 60). The optional rule-based query rewriter (dashed) issues spelling, orthographic and affix-spacing variants whose BM25 rankings are fused with weighted RRF (original query weight 2.0; variants share weight 1.0).

**Figure 2 — `fig2_perturbation_workflow.(png|pdf)`.** Perturbation-generation workflow (replaces Algorithm 1). Each clean query is transformed by T_char, T_zwnj and T_typo, and by their composition T_comp = T_typo ∘ T_zwnj ∘ T_char; T_typo and T_comp are additionally generated on an EditRate grid {0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.30}. T_real replaces words by spelling variants attested in the passage collection. Every operator uses a private random generator seeded with SHA-256(42 | query id | operator | EditRate), which makes the perturbed query sets order-independent and exactly reproducible. Only queries are perturbed; passages are left unchanged.

**Figure 3 — `fig3_perturbation_taxonomy.(png|pdf)`.** Perturbation taxonomy with Persian examples. (a) Output of every operator for one MIRACL-fa dev query (id 4809078#0), copied from the generated query sets. (b) Examples of orthographic alternations whose both forms occur at least five times in the MIRACL-fa passage pool; such attested pairs define the realistic-noise condition T_real.

**Figure 4 — `fig4_degradation_<collection>.(png|pdf)`.** Within-system degradation ΔnDCG@10 = nDCG@10(T(q)) − nDCG@10(q) for each system and perturbation type. Error bars: 95% bootstrap confidence intervals of the mean paired difference (10,000 resamples; cluster bootstrap over paragraphs for PQuAD). † T_zwnj and T_real are computed on the queries the operator actually changed (e.g., 62 and 329 of 632 MIRACL-fa queries), because the remaining queries are unchanged by construction. Variants: `miracl` (100k-passage pool), `miracl30k` (30k pool, includes BGE-M3 and mE5-base), `pquad`, `gold`.

**Figure 5 — `fig5_dose_response_<collection>.(png|pdf)`.** Dose–response: nDCG@10 as a function of the per-token edit probability (EditRate) for T_typo (left) and T_comp (right). EditRate = 0 is the clean query. The large drop of unnormalised BM25 at the first T_comp point is caused by the T_char component, not by typos.

**Figure 6 — `fig6_retention_heatmap_<collection>.(png|pdf)`.** Relative retention ratio R = nDCG@10(noisy) / nDCG@10(clean) per system (rows) and perturbation (columns). For T_char, T_typo and T_comp R is computed over all queries; for columns marked † over the queries the operator changed. R = 1 means no loss; R > 1 means the perturbed query scored higher.

**Figure 7 — `fig7_dataset_statistics.(png|pdf)`.** Collection and perturbation statistics. (a) Query-length distributions (whitespace tokens) for MIRACL-fa dev, PQuAD validation and the gold benchmark. (b) Share of queries changed by each operator at EditRate 0.15; T_zwnj changes only queries that contain a ZWNJ (10% of MIRACL-fa, 52% of PQuAD, 0% of the gold queries). (c) Number of words in the MIRACL-fa pool with at least one attested variant, by alternation type (log scale).
